Rental & support

Your Inventory. Your Sales. Our Support.

Maybel’s rental and support service makes it straightforward to add beer and alcohol vending machines to your location.

Your team stocks the machine with your inventory and retains the drink sales revenue. Maybel handles equipment monitoring, technical support, parts, repairs and servicing—including support for guests with machine or payment issues.

Each machine has its own cellular connection, so no connection to your location’s network is required. Your team stays responsible for alcohol service; we take care of the technology.

Responsible service

Self-Service, Built Around Local Requirements.

Maybel’s access options are tailored to your location and jurisdiction. Our core approach in Canada and the U.S. lets staff authorize access so guests can then choose and purchase drinks themselves, without staff assistance for each purchase.

This human-authorized model enables legal self-service in Ontario licensed establishments and Quebec hotels without relying on facial recognition. Maybel also supports ID scanning where permitted, including Nova Scotia and Newfoundland and Labrador.

Configurable purchase limits, automatic operating-hour restrictions, transaction records and remote disabling help your team maintain oversight.
